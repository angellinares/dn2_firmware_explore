Digitone II Mods / composer

Mod Composer

Tick the mods that you want. The page checks which ones combine and builds one file. It also shows the command that builds the same file from the repository. Three mods take your own files: audio for the transients, wavetables for the LFO waves and a picture for the boot screen. When you tick one of them, its files open below it. Each mod's own page is its manual.

Make sure that you can go back to stock before you flash a modified file. The Early Start-up Menu flashes the stock firmware over MIDI. Do this one time with the stock file, before you flash a modified file. Back up your +Drive.

Go to 1.11 from 1.10E. If your unit has OS 1.10D, install the stock OS 1.10E first. Then install OS 1.11, stock or modified. Do not go from 1.10D directly to 1.11.

      Your transients: 0 of 32 slots replaced
      
    
  
      
  
      Choose a slot, then drop audio on it or choose a file. You can use any format and rate that
  your browser can play. The page mixes the audio to mono, resamples it to 48 kHz and fits it
  to the length of the slot. Click the waveform to hear it.
      
  
      
    
    
      Off (the default): each slot keeps its factory length: 50, 100 or
    150 ms. Choose a slot that is long enough for your sample. The factory transients that you
    do not replace sound exactly as before.
      
    
      On: every slot is 107.6 ms, 5,164 samples. Every sample gets the same
    space. But the build changes the factory transients that you do not replace. It cuts the 8 of
    150 ms to 107.6 ms, with a short fade. It pads the other 24 with silence: the 3 of
    50 ms, and the 21 of 100 ms by 7.6 ms each. The slots below mark them.
      
    
      You can change this before you build. The page fits your samples again to the new lengths.
