Reload Confirm

FUNC + NO reloads the pattern at once, and one stray press loses the work since your last save. With this mod it can ask first. Switch it on in SETTINGS > PERSONALIZE, and off again when you perform. Select your firmware file. Then download a signed image.

Make sure that you can go back to stock before you flash a modified file. The Early Start-up Menu flashes the stock firmware over MIDI. Do this one time with the stock file, before you flash a modified file. Back up your +Drive. No tool here can recover it.

What changes

The switch
SETTINGS > PERSONALIZE > RELOAD CONFIRM, the last item. Off by default, so a freshly flashed instrument behaves as stock
On
FUNC + NO opens a prompt, ARE YOU SURE YOU WANT TO / RELOAD THE PATTERN? Y/N. YES reloads the pattern, as FUNC + NO always does. NO keeps your edits
Off
FUNC + NO reloads at once, as stock: for performing
Kept
The switch is saved with your other PERSONALIZE settings and survives a power cycle, with or without SAVE PROJECT
Not covered
The other reloads (PARAMETER + NO, TRK + TRIG + NO) and the pattern menu’s own RELOAD, which already asks
Writes
—

Confirmed on a Digitone II, 2026-10-04. With the switch off, FUNC + NO reloads at once. With the switch on, the prompt appears: NO keeps the edits and YES reverts them. The switch stays on after a power cycle without a save. A flash of the stock firmware back causes no problem: the stock firmware never reads the switch.
