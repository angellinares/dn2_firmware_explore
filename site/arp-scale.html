Arp Offsets in Scale

The arpeggiator adds each step’s offset in semitones, so an offset can take a note out of the track’s scale. With this mod, the offsets can count degrees of the scale instead, and you can mark single steps to stay in semitones. Select your firmware file. Then download a signed image.

Make sure that you can go back to stock before you flash a modified file. The Early Start-up Menu flashes the stock firmware over MIDI. Do this one time with the stock file, before you flash a modified file. Back up your +Drive. No tool here can recover it.

How to use it

  1. Turn it on. In SETTINGS > PERSONALIZE, set ARP OFS IN SCALE to on. Each offset now counts degrees of the track’s scale, from its root, as KEYBOARD SETUP sets them. In C major, E plus 2 plays G, not F#.
  2. Mark a step to move in semitones. Press [ARP] to open the ARPEGGIATOR menu, and select a step with [LEFT] or [RIGHT]. On an active step, press [UP]: the step shows a plus, and its offset counts semitones, for a note outside the scale. [DOWN] removes the mark, and [DOWN] again mutes the step.

With ARP OFS IN SCALE off, the arpeggiator is stock: every step moves in semitones, and the menu shows no plus. The marks stay with the sound for when you turn it on again.

Version and changes

This mod is at v0.2.0. What changed in each version, newest first.

v0.2.0 2026-10-25
  • With ARP OFS IN SCALE on, you can mark an arp step to move in semitones, for a note outside the scale. In the ARPEGGIATOR menu, press UP on an active step: the step shows a plus. Press DOWN to remove the mark, and DOWN again to mute the step.
  • The sound keeps its marks when you save it, copy it, or copy, paste and clear its arp. With ARP OFS IN SCALE off, every step moves in semitones, as before.
  • You can now build it on the site, on this page.

What changes

The switch
SETTINGS > PERSONALIZE > ARP OFS IN SCALE. Off by default, so a freshly flashed instrument behaves as stock
On
An offset counts degrees of the track’s scale. An offset of 0 plays the held note, even one outside the scale. Other offsets first move a note outside the scale to the nearest note in it. RANGE still adds whole octaves
Marked steps
With the switch on, [UP] on an active step in the ARPEGGIATOR menu marks it, and its offset counts semitones. The marks are saved with the sound, and the menu’s rotate, copy, paste and clear move them with the steps
Off
Stock: every step counts semitones, marked or not, and the menu works and looks as stock
Semitones anyway
SCALE CHROMATIC, and MODE CHORD MEMORY in KEYBOARD SETUP
Kept
The switch is saved with your other PERSONALIZE settings and survives a power cycle, with or without SAVE PROJECT
Writes
—

Confirmed on a Digitone II, 2026-10-14, before marked steps existed. With the switch on, the arp plays in scale, from trigs and from keys held on the KEYBOARD page. With it off, the arp plays stock’s notes. The switch stays on after a power cycle.

Marked steps (version 0.2.0) passed on w-winter’s Digitone II, 2026-10-25: a marked step plays in semitones, and the marks survive a power cycle and a project reload.
