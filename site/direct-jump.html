Digitone II Mods / direct jump

Direct Jump

On stock firmware, a pattern you cue starts when the playing pattern ends. With this mod, it can start on the next step. It goes on from where the playing pattern was, as on the Analog Rytm with DIRECT JUMP. Select your firmware file. Then download a signed image.

Make sure that you can go back to stock before you flash a modified file. The Early Start-up Menu flashes the stock firmware over MIDI. Do this one time with the stock file, before you flash a modified file. Back up your +Drive. No tool here can recover it.

Go to 1.11 from 1.10E. If your unit has OS 1.10D, install the stock OS 1.10E first. Then install OS 1.11, stock or modified. Do not go from 1.10D directly to 1.11.

How to use it

  1. Turn it on. Press [PTN] to open the pattern view, or hold it. Then press [UP]. The title bar shows “Direct Jump ON”. While it is on, [PTN] lights magenta in the pattern view, as the [TRIG] keys do in PATTERN MUTE mode. [DOWN] turns it off and shows “Direct Jump OFF”.
  2. Cue a pattern. While the sequencer plays, press [PTN] and a [TRIG] key, as on stock firmware. The new pattern starts on the next step. If you cue late in a step, it starts one step later. It goes on from the position of the playing pattern. For example, if A01 is on its step 11 and A02 has 8 steps, A02 goes on from its step 3.

Chains, songs and MIDI program changes change at the end of the pattern, as on stock firmware, unless one comes between a jump’s cue and the jump. The jump takes the newest cue. With the switch off, every change is stock’s.

What changes

The switch
In the pattern view, [UP] turns DIRECT JUMP on and [DOWN] turns it off. Off by default, so an instrument that has never had it on behaves as stock. The mod takes a cue made with the switch on within about 40 ms. After that, the jump goes ahead even if you turn the switch off
On
A pattern cued in the pattern view while the sequencer plays starts on the next step. At 1x, a cue in the last third of a step starts on the step after. The pattern goes on from the playing pattern’s position, wrapped by its own length. In per-track mode, each track wraps by its own length
Page Loop
The position is the full pattern’s, which the top row of [TRIG] keys shows
LST
A jump cuts the last pass short. Trigs with LST do not play, and trigs with !LST play. A cue too late for the next step lets the old pattern play one more step as its last pass
Stock anyway
Chains, songs, MIDI program changes, and a cue while the sequencer is stopped. One that comes between a jump’s cue and the jump is taken by the jump
Kept
The instrument saves the switch with its settings, as it saves the switches of Reload confirm and Arp offsets in scale. So the switch survives a power cycle without SAVE PROJECT
Writes

Not yet tested by the repository owner. On w-winter’s Digitone II, 2026-11-12, the jumps, the switch, the arp, a heavy load and a power cycle worked. Checked in the emulator, 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12. Every cue started on the expected step, at the expected position. The checks included patterns of different lengths, Page Loop and per-track mode. With the switch off, and for both ways to make a chain, the notes were the same as stock’s.
