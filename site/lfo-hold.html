LFO Hold

An LFO in TRIG MODE ONE should run one cycle and stop; in HALF, half a cycle and stop. On the factory firmware it stops, but then jumps to the centre: a square LFO on the filter “turns off” after one cycle, or “jumps back to the middle”. This fix makes it hold the value it reached. Pick your firmware, download a signed image.

What changes

ONE
After one cycle the LFO stays at the value its waveform ended on, instead of jumping to the centre. A square on the filter ends closed and stays closed
HALF
After half a cycle the LFO stays at the waveform’s middle value. A square stays open; a triangle stays at its peak
Unchanged
FREE, TRIG and HOLD; the random waveform, which already kept its last value; the next trig, which restarts the LFO as before
Why
The firmware stops these modes by writing a fixed value per waveform into the LFO’s output, the centre for nearly all of them. The fix removes those two writes, so the output keeps the last value it computed
Writes
—

Confirmed on a Digitone II, 2026-10-08, against factory 1.11 on the same patch (a square LFO on the filter): ONE and HALF hold with the fix and jump to the middle without it. The click you may hear at a square’s edges is the factory’s, the same with and without the fix. Reported by the community on official 1.10 as well.
