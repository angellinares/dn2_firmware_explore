Digitone II Mods / transients

Transient Swapper

Replace any of the Digitone II's 32 FM DRUM transients with your own samples. Select your firmware file. Drop in your audio. Listen to the result. Then download a signed image.

Make sure that you can go back to stock before you flash a modified file. The Early Start-up Menu flashes the stock firmware over MIDI. Do this one time with the stock file, before you flash a modified file. Back up your +Drive. No tool here can recover it.

Version and changes

This mod is at v2.0.0. What changed in each version, newest first.

v2.0.0 2026-10-24
  • Every slot now plays your whole sample at its TRAN value. Before, a slot below TRAN 52 could play part of your sample, or part of the next one.
  • There are now 32 slots, one for each TRAN step. Slot n plays at TRAN 4 x n.
  • Each slot keeps its factory length, 50, 100 or 150 ms, and the page shows it. The factory transients that you do not replace sound as before.
  • A new option, Equal slots, makes every slot 107.6 ms. It cuts 8 factory transients from 150 ms and pads the other 24 with silence.
  • A bank made with 1.0.0 has its samples in other slots. Build it again with this version.
  • Works on a firmware with Waverider, in either order.
v1.0.0 2026-10-11
  • First numbered version.

The bank

32 slots, one for each TRAN step. Click a waveform to hear it. To replace a slot, drop audio on it. You can use any format and rate that your browser can play. The page mixes the audio to mono and resamples it for you.

Off (the default): each slot keeps its factory length. The factory slots are 50, 100 or 150 ms, and each slot below shows its length. Choose a slot that is long enough for your sample. The factory transients that you do not replace sound exactly as before.

On: every slot is 107.6 ms (5,164 samples at 48 kHz). Every sample gets the same space. But the build changes the factory transients that you do not replace: it cuts the 8 of 150 ms to 107.6 ms, with a short fade, and it pads the other 24 with silence: the 3 of 50 ms, and the 21 of 100 ms by 7.6 ms each. Each of those slots says so below.

You can change this before you build. Your samples are fitted again to the new lengths.

Your sample is cut to fit its slot. The page truncates a longer sample. It pads a shorter sample with silence at the end. If the page cuts your file, the slot says so and gives the original length. The start control moves the part that the page keeps.

The waveform of the slot shows your whole file, with the imported window shaded. So you can see exactly what the page keeps and what it drops. For a short sample, you can also see how much of the slot is silence.

Slot n plays at TRAN = 4 × n. Slot 0 is at TRAN 0, slot 31 at TRAN 124. Every slot below shows its value.

Values between two slots blend the two slots. How this was measured.
