Parameter Slides

On stock firmware, a parameter lock changes its parameter at once, when its trig plays. With this mod, a trig can glide to its locks over a length that you set. It can also glide on to the values of the next trig. Select your firmware file. Then download a signed image.

Make sure that you can go back to stock before you flash a modified file. The Early Start-up Menu flashes the stock firmware over MIDI. Do this one time with the stock file, before you flash a modified file. Back up your +Drive. No tool here can recover it.

Go to 1.11 from 1.10E. If your unit has OS 1.10D, install the stock OS 1.10E first. Then install OS 1.11, stock or modified. Do not go from 1.10D directly to 1.11.

How to use it

  1. Open SLD (slide). In GRID RECORDING mode, press [TRIG PARAMETERS] twice to open TRIG PAGE 2. SLD is under RTRG.
  2. Set it on a trig. Hold one or more [TRIG] keys and turn DATA ENTRY knob E. Choose a length of 1/2 to 16 steps, or NEXT. With no [TRIG] key held, SLD shows OFF and knob E does nothing. A value other than OFF shows inverted, as a locked parameter does. To set SLD back to OFF, hold the [TRIG] keys and press knob E.
  3. Lock parameters on the trig, as on stock firmware. With a length, the trig glides to its locks over that many steps. With NEXT, it glides from its locks to the next trig’s values.

What changes

SLD
TRIG PAGE 2 shows SLD under RTRG, on DATA ENTRY knob E: OFF, 1/2, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 or NEXT. A trig with SLD OFF changes its locks at once, as on stock. Until a trig has SLD set, the instrument behaves as stock
A length
The trig glides to its locks over that many steps of the track’s speed. Each parameter starts from the value the track had just before the trig. A parameter that the trig before locked, and this trig does not lock, glides back to the preset’s value
NEXT
The trig glides from its own locks to the next trig’s values, and arrives as the next trig plays. The next trig is the next note trig or lock trig in the track’s length, or in the looped pages when Page Loop is on. If only one of the two trigs locks a parameter, the other end is the preset’s value. The trig condition of the next trig does not count, and its micro timing does not change the time
Parameters
A parameter glides when an LFO can modulate it and its range is wide. Frequencies, levels, envelope times and sends glide. SPD, FADE, SPH and DEP of LFO 1 to 3 glide too. ALGO, switches and other parameters with few values change at once. Some, such as the amp ATK, take effect only when a note starts, so their slide is heard from the next note
Voices
Each note glides on its own voice. Retrigs, chord notes and arp notes that start during the slide glide with it. On a lock trig, every voice of the track glides, each from its own value. When a new slide starts on a voice, it starts where the earlier slide was. A later lock of the same parameter on a trig without SLD takes the parameter out of the slide
During a slide
Turn a knob, and its parameter stops the slide and follows the knob. [STOP], a pattern change and a jump with the Direct Jump mod stop the slide where it is. A jump that starts a shorter pattern on its first step stops the slide a step late. A tempo change changes the rest of the slide, which still ends on its value. A trig with a preset lock of another machine changes at once, with a length or with NEXT. So does a lock trig on a voice that plays another machine. A trig that does not play, on a muted track or because of its condition, starts no slide
Kept
The pattern keeps SLD with its trig. Trig copy and paste, page and track paste, and FREEZE TRACK keep it. Stock firmware plays the pattern with each lock changing at once
