Digitone II Mods / transients

Transient Swapper

Replace any of the Digitone II's 34 FM DRUM transients with your own samples. Select your firmware file. Drop in your audio. Listen to the result. Then download a signed image.

Make sure that you can go back to stock before you flash a modified file. The Early Start-up Menu flashes the stock firmware over MIDI. Do this one time with the stock file, before you flash a modified file. Back up your +Drive. No tool here can recover it.

The bank

34 slots. Click a waveform to hear it. To replace a slot, drop audio on it. You can use any format and rate that your browser can play. The page mixes the audio to mono and resamples it for you.

Every slot is exactly 100 ms, and your sample is cut to fit. That is 4,800 samples at 48 kHz, the same for all 34. There is no length field to change.

The page truncates a longer sample. It pads a shorter sample with silence at the end. If the page cuts your file, the slot says so and gives the original length. The start control moves the 100 ms that the page keeps.

The waveform of the slot shows your whole file, with the imported window shaded. So you can see exactly what the page keeps and what it drops. For a short sample, you can also see how much of the slot is silence.

The transients of Elektron do not all fit either: 20 of the 34 still ring when their slot ends. You hear that ring at the start of the next slot. So the factory bank behaves like a continuous 3.4-second recording, cut into 100 ms pieces, and not like 34 separate hits. When you replace a slot, the opening of the next slot also changes, because the borrowed tail goes away. A sample that decays inside 100 ms behaves more predictably than the factory content does.

Each slot sits at TRAN = 4 × slot − 8, measured on hardware. Slot 20 is at TRAN 72, slot 33 at TRAN 124. Every slot below shows its value.

You cannot select slots 0 and 1. TRAN starts at 0, which is already slot 2. So the first two entries are in the bank, but the knob cannot reach them. Do not put a sample there. The knob reaches 32 of the 34.

The engine also interpolates between neighbours over that four-unit stride, so values between two slots blend them rather than snapping. How this was measured.

Build it

The image is rebuilt, re-signed with the key derived from your own file, and every integrity field is checked again. Nothing downloads unless all of them pass.
