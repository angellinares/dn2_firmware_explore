LFO Hold

The first fix for LFO TRIG MODE ONE and HALF. It is kept here as a record: what it did, how it was tested, and why it was replaced. It is no longer offered as a download.

Superseded on 2026-10-09 by the LFO ONE/HALF fix. Two things came to light after this fix was published:

  • The MIDI tracks’ LFOs run through a second copy of the LFO code, which this fix did not touch. On a MIDI track, ONE and HALF still jumped when they stopped.
  • A second community report: with the start phase (SPH) moved, ONE and HALF end too early, or in HALF too late. The firmware stops them at fixed points of the cycle, not one cycle (or half) after the trig. Source

The LFO ONE/HALF fix does what this one did, on both copies, and fixes the length too. The two write the same bytes, so they were never meant to be combined.

What it did

Fixed
LFO TRIG MODE ONE and HALF jumping to the centre when they stop, instead of holding the value they reached
ONE
After one cycle the LFO stayed at the value its waveform ended on. A square on the filter ended closed and stayed closed
HALF
After half a cycle the LFO stayed at the waveform’s middle value. A square stayed open; a triangle stayed at its peak
Why
The firmware stops these modes by writing a fixed value per waveform into the LFO’s output, the centre for nearly all of them. The fix removed those two writes (audio tracks)
Changes
2 in-place edits of 4 B in section 3, nothing appended
Tested
Flashed 2026-10-08 against factory 1.11 on the same patch (a square LFO on the filter): ONE and HALF held with the fix and jumped without it; TRIG and FREE unchanged; the click at a square’s edges was the factory’s, the same with and without the fix
Code
src/dnfw/mods/lfohold.py and site/js/mods/lfohold.js in the repository, kept as published

Reported by the community on official 1.10 as well. Source
