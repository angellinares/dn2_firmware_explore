Layering to MIDI

Layer an audio track onto MIDI tracks with TRACK WILL TRIGGER, and the MIDI tracks play along over MIDI: every note, chord and release your audio track plays. Pick your firmware, download a signed image.

Everything happens in this tab. Your firmware file is never uploaded — this page has no server to upload it to. It is static files on GitHub Pages; you can read every line in the repository.

Prove your way back before you flash anything. The Early Start-up Menu reflashes stock firmware over MIDI. Do that once, with an unmodified image, before you flash a modified one — and back up your +Drive, which no tool here can recover.

2

What changes

Set up
On the audio track, TRACK WILL TRIGGER: select one or more MIDI tracks
Playback
Every note the audio track plays — trigs, chords, retrigs, probability, held keys — also goes out on each layered MIDI track's channel
Releases
Note-off when the layered note ends: at its length, when a held key is let go, or at stop for INF
Combining
Several audio tracks can layer onto one MIDI track, and one audio track onto several
Mutes
A muted source sends nothing; a muted MIDI track sends nothing
Not layered
Every track behaves as stock
Writes
—

Confirmed on a Digitone II, 2026-10-02, recorded into Ableton from an external synth on the layered MIDI track: sequenced notes, chords, retrigs and probability arrive as played; INF holds until the sequencer stops; held keys release cleanly; two sources into one MIDI track and one source into several both work. The MIDI note arrived about 8 ms ahead of the recorded audio — the audio path's latency, not the mod's.

Build it

The image is rebuilt, re-signed with the key derived from your own file, and every integrity field is checked again. Nothing downloads unless all of them pass.

Not with the MIDI Arpeggiator

MIDI Arpeggiator also turns layered notes on MIDI tracks into MIDI, when the MIDI track's own arp is on. The two no longer share any bytes, but until that overlap is checked they are kept apart: they cannot be built together.
