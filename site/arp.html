MIDI Arpeggiator

Use the Digitone II arpeggiator on MIDI tracks. The ARPEGGIATOR menu opens, and the arp plays your external gear over MIDI. Select your firmware file. Then download a signed image.

Make sure that you can go back to stock before you flash a modified file. The Early Start-up Menu flashes the stock firmware over MIDI. Do this one time with the stock file, before you flash a modified file. Back up your +Drive. No tool here can recover it.

What changes

Menu
[FUNC]+[ARP] opens the ARPEGGIATOR menu on a MIDI track, and its knobs stay on it
Playback
With the arp on, the track's trigs and held keys arpeggiate and go out on the track's MIDI channel
Follows
MODE, SPD, RNG, N.LEN, LEN, the step mask and note offsets: the settings that a synth track uses
Arp off
The MIDI track behaves as stock. The synth tracks do not change
Layering
A track layered onto a MIDI track (TRACK WILL TRIGGER) plays there only through the arp of that MIDI track. With the arp off, it stays silent, as on stock. It never sends a wrong note
Busy MIDI
If the MIDI task's 64 note records run short, an arp note is dropped rather than risk the instrument freezing
Writes
—

Confirmed on a Digitone II, 2026-09-17, captured from its MIDI output at 120 BPM with SPD 1/16. Offsets and velocity came through, steps arrived every 125 ms, and no note stuck across 5,248 note messages. Note lengths follow N.LEN:

Build it

The image is rebuilt, re-signed with the key derived from your own file, and every integrity field is checked again. Nothing downloads unless all of them pass.

Combining with other mods

Each page applies one mod. To combine mods, build here. Then load the downloaded file into the next page, in any order. This mod writes a few edits inside section 3. Its code goes into the appended area of the platform: a 592 byte block that the start-up copies to RAM. The other mods that use the platform share that area.

  • LFO Waves: combines. No shared bytes, and neither changes the other's code.
  • Modulation Destinations: combines.
  • Transient Swapper: combines. It only writes the DSP image.
  • A Fourth LFO: combines. Build it last, as it asks.

What "combines" means here. A check makes sure that the bytes of the two mods do not overlap. A chain of all three section 3 mods rebuilds and passes its checks, with the same result in the browser as on the command line. The arpeggiator passed on hardware alone. Nobody has flashed a combined image yet.
